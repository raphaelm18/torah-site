One. Shaddai. On the hand, you're wearing the name Shaddai.

Two. Shin, dalet, yud. The letters shin, dalet, and yud spell Shaddai.

Three. Megillah. In the Megillah of Esther, the people took the Torah on again.

Four. Purim. Like Purim, you're taking it on again, this time by choice.

Five. Shema. In the Shema, we bind these words as a sign on the hand.

Six. Mezuzah. The mezuzah is the same, with Shaddai on the back of the scroll.

Seven. Yeshiva. You still see yeshiva students in Jerusalem, in tefillin all day.
