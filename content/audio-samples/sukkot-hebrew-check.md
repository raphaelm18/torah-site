Pronunciation check for the Sukkot episode. Each word comes after its number.

One. Sukkot. On Sukkot, we leave the house.

Two. Sukkah. We sat in the sukkah on Wednesday night.

Three. Kohelet. King Shlomo wrote Kohelet.

Four. Hevel. Hevel havalim, hakol havel.

Five. Lulav. The lulav we shake all week comes from a date palm.

Six. Schach. There was no ceiling over us, just the schach.

Seven. Tanakh. There's no one in the Tanakh who is joyful by himself.

Eight. Sameach. No one in the Tanakh is sameach by himself.

Nine. Simcha. Kohelet's answer is simcha.

Ten. Yirat shamayim. A quality project has some yirat shamayim in it.

Eleven. Shivat haminim. The date is one of the shivat haminim.

Twelve. The blessing. Baruch atah Hashem, Elokeinu melech ha'olam, borei pri ha'etz.

Thirteen. The verse. Lech echol b'simcha lachmecha.

Fourteen. Chag Sameach.

Fifteen. A jalapeño cheddar bagel with tuna from Mission Bagel.
